## Fios do redesenho (V1N00–V1N08)

*Threads of the redesign.* Four threads run through the first nine lines. Each one is born
as the image of a word in the speech and comes back later, transformed: that is what keeps
the frame moving on the sentences that carry the idea, and what keeps the picture from
contradicting the speech.

- **The casino chip (the bet).** A small casino chip, drawn for the series: a disc with an inner
  ring and marks around the edge, in **ROXO** — the only colour in `paleta.py` with no role
  in video 1 (never ROSA, VERDE2, LARANJA or CIANO, which already mean `p`, `q`, `n` and
  quantum). It enters in V1N01 and rests at the foot of the padlock (it hops to the arrow
  lane on "the bet is" and comes back in V1N02); it cracks in V1N06; it leaves with the
  remains in V1N08.
- **The way back.** Black: it advances in jolts and shatters in V1N01, hits the wall of
  digits and shatters in V1N02. The ordinary computer tries brute force in V1N03 (the row
  of crossed-out divisions) and never finishes. In V1N05 it is reborn in CIANO and gets
  through.
- **Time.** Progress bar in V1N02 ("in a reasonable time") → the years counter in V1N03 →
  the counter collapses down to "hours" in V1N05 → the timeline with the dashed stretch up
  to "?" in V1N06 ("expiration date") → the envelope travels along that dashed stretch in
  V1N07. Always a line, never a circular dial (rule 1 of `CLAUDE.md`).
- **Grey × cyan.** Grey is classical: the ordinary processor, the question marks the failing
  arrow shatters into. Cyan is quantum: the qubits, the arrow that gets through, the
  processor in V1N05, "hours", the pulse through the network in V1N06 and "Shor" in the title in
  V1N08.

### Pieces and notation

- **The V1N04 labels are formulas**, not text: `formula()` from `ferramentas.py`, with no
  key in `textos.py` (notation stays in code). `p·q` = `formula(("p", ROSA), ("·", PRETO),
  ("q", VERDE2))`; `mod n` = `formula(("mod", PRETO), ("n", LARANJA))` (without the
  parentheses of `MOD()`); `φ(n)` = `formula(("φ(", PRETO), ("n", LARANJA), (")", PRETO))`,
  the same tokens as `capitulo8.py`; `e·d` = `formula(("e", PRETO), ("·", PRETO), ("d",
  PRETO))`. None has an exponent, so `pot()` is not used. `e·d` stays black, not red and
  blue as in chapter 8, because purple belongs to the chip and red, in this stretch, is the
  strike of failure.
- **New on-screen texts** (`shor/textos.py`): `v1.diffie_hellman`, `v1.curvas_elipticas`,
  `v1.horas`. Numbers and symbols ("1994", "?", "÷2", the long primes, the wall of digits)
  stay in code.
- **Existing pieces reused:** `_qubit()` (V1N00, V1N06, V1N07), `_pacote()`/`_envelope()`
  (V1N07), `_contador()` (V1N03, V1N05), the forward and backward arrows of V1N01, the
  primes `p` and `q` of V1N02. New pieces, as `_` functions in `video1.py`: the casino chip, the
  processor, the wall of digits, the progress bar, the sheet of paper and the open mini
  padlock.

## Critério das prévias

Barra, seta, tabela, árvore e movimento. Fórmula só como pouso de um trecho, nunca como
assunto dele, e nenhuma cascata de equações se transformando — isso é o corpo dos outros
vídeos, e aqui espantaria quem ainda está decidindo se fica. Quando a miniatura tem uma
equação fixa no topo (é o caso dos capítulos 2, 3, 4 e 5), o enquadramento da janela corta
essa faixa e mostra só as retas.

Os tempos são relativos ao início de cada locução e admitem folga: o último trecho de cada
prévia pode invadir o `PAD`, porque o corte para o título seguinte só acontece no primeiro
quadro da fala seguinte.
